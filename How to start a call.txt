STARTING A GAME
1. Main menu: PLAY, then HOST A GAME, then INVITE ONLY or PUBLIC, then Campaign.

STARTING THE DAY
1. Press N. It sprints you to the door that starts the day.
   (Or press ] until you hear "Door, Continue Day 1", then Backspace.)
2. When you hear "Arrived", hold E for a second or two. This starts the workday.
   If the day is already running, N tells you so. Then press M instead.

SITTING AT YOUR DESK
Chairs in the lobby and break room are not desks. You need a "Work computer".
A "Lobby computer" only has Character Creator, Caller Manager and Game Manager, and never gets calls.
1. Press M. It walks you to the nearest free work computer.
   If there is none on this floor, it takes you to the floor entrance door instead. Hold E there, then press M again.
   (Or press ] until you hear "Work computer", then Backspace.)
2. It sprints you to that desk's chair, sits you down and opens the computer by itself.
   You will hear "Computer, Desktop".
   If it only says "Arrived", press E while standing.
3. If you are already sitting in a desk chair, just press E.
   It opens that desk's computer, wherever you are facing.

GETTING YOUR FIRST SCAM APP (free, only once)
1. On the desktop, use the Down arrow to find Scamazon and press Enter.
2. Arrow to the Gift Card or Tech Support app and press Enter on its button.

TAKING A CALL
1. Wait at the computer. When it rings you will hear "Incoming call from ...".
2. Press Enter to answer. Press Down arrow then Enter to decline instead.
3. Talk out loud into your microphone. The caller answers with a voice.
4. If you find a text box in the Phone window, press Enter on it, type your message, and press Enter to send.
5. Use the scam app's Script to ask the caller for the listed details,
   then type each answer into the matching box in that app.
6. When you are done, find Hang up in the Phone window and press Enter.

KEYS WHILE WALKING
Up / Down arrow (or W / S)   walk forward / back
Left / Right arrow           turn (Shift + arrow turns 90 degrees)
A / D                        step sideways
[ and ]                      previous / next nearby object (turns you to face it)
Backspace                    auto walk to the selected object (stands you up if sitting)
M                            sprint to the nearest free work computer and sit at it
N                            sprint to the door that starts the work day
Backslash                    where is the selected object
Home                         look straight ahead
E                            interact (hold E for doors)
Q / Z                        other interact actions
; (semicolon)                left click action
' (apostrophe)               right click action
Space                        jump / stand up from a chair
F3                           wall sounds on or off
F4                           object pings on or off

KEYS AT THE COMPUTER
Up / Down arrow    move through the window
Enter              open or press the selected item, or start typing in a box
Left / Right       change a dropdown or slider
Tab / Shift+Tab    switch between open windows and the desktop
Home / End         first / last item
F1                 computer help
Escape             leave the computer

KEYS ANYWHERE
F5    status: your money, the quota, time left, and the current training step
F9    read the whole screen or window
F10   repeat the last thing said
F11   set the game and call language to English
